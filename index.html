Welcome

I am a mathematician with research interests in mathematical modeling of biological systems, particularly focusing on reaction-diffusion equations, pattern formation, and bulk-surface dynamics. My work combines analytical techniques, numerical methods, and computational approaches to understand complex phenomena in cellular biology and population dynamics.

Research Interests

Pattern Formation and Reaction-Diffusion Systems

I study the formation of spatial patterns arising from reaction-diffusion systems, with applications to biological phenomena such as cell polarization, bacterial community dynamics, and predator-prey interactions.

Fractional Calculus and Memory Effects

My research explores fractional derivatives and their applications in biomathematical models, enabling the incorporation of memory effects and non-local interactions in mathematical models of biological systems.

Bulk-Surface Models

I develop and analyze coupled bulk-surface models that capture the interaction between processes occurring in the interior of cells and on their membranes, with applications to cell polarization and morphogenesis.
